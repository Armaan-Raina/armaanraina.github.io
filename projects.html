Coursework

A collection of the most interesting courses I've taken thus far in my undergraduate degree.

ECE 505

Neural Interface Engineering

Biophysics, stimulation/recording electrode design, and existing applications (pacemakers, retinal implants, DBS, etc.).
Main takeaway: material scientists are unbelievably underappreciated.

ECE 492

Physical AI with Brain-Inspired Electronics

Advances in neuromorphic computing, leveraging physical systems to perform computation, and unique considerations for on-chip computing.
Main takeaway: why does physics keep showing up everywhere?

ST 422

Mathematical Statistics II

Estimators and how to evaluate their quality, plus an in-depth revisit of confidence intervals and hypothesis testing.
Main takeaway: a new perspective on inference and how to think about the quality of guesses.

BIO 488

Neurobiology

Ionic basis of the action potential, synaptic plasticity, motor pathways, and the neural basis for several disorders.
Main takeaway: plasticity is an extremely powerful concept.
