Hi, I'm Armaan.

I'm a fourth-year computer science and statistics student at NC State fascinated by the human brain. I've dedicated my time in undergrad to applying computational methods to learn more about and build devices based on neural signals. From applications of machine learning in single cell recordings to closed-loop stimulation devices, I've had the privilege of working on a variety of problems all of which are inspired by my curiosity about the complexity of our brains.
